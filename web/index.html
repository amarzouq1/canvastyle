Methodology & anchors

How the virtual patients work

Each virtual patient draws a personal net weekly tumor log-growth rate: mu_i ~ Normal(arm efficacy, heterogeneity), then the burden evolves weekly with noise. Outcomes at week 12 use RECIST-style thresholds on the burden index (PR ≤ 70, PD > 120). Arm efficacies are calibrated to published anchors:

• ICI + ivermectin: 6/15 complete regressions in the 4T1 model with statistical synergy (npj Breast Cancer 2021) · metformin: HR 0.66 for progression in 243 MIBC patients after cystectomy + gemcitabine/cisplatin (BMC Cancer 2026), synergy in bladder cell lines/xenografts · docetaxel + ivermectin: HSP27 mechanism (JCI 2022) with a randomized bladder trial behind the target · ivermectin alone: bladder xenograft growth inhibition (2024) · GC and ICI single agents: standard urothelial response rates.

• NMIBC anchors: BCG alone ~55–60% historical CR; BCG + intravesical gemcitabine 95% CR at 6 months (phase I/II, NCT04179162); BCG + systemic ICI ~73% 12-month CRR with grade ≥3 AEs in 25% (RCT meta-analysis); BCG + curcumin from syngeneic bladder-tumor synergy (Cancer Res 2009).

• Limits: the anchors drive everything; no PK modeling; toxicity enters only via the safety score. Open-source simulation code (Python) in the companion GitHub repo. The honest conclusion survives most perturbations: ivermectin is a combination partner, not a solo agent; ivermectin + checkpoint inhibition has the deepest foundation; metformin is the best-supported oral companion.

Safety — do not self-medicate
Ivermectin is a CYP3A4/P-gp substrate; HCQ carries QT and retinal risks; metformin carries GI, B12 and rare lactic-acidosis risk (especially with nephrotoxic cisplatin). Oncology trial doses of ivermectin (30–60 mg) far exceed antiparasitic dosing. Any use belongs under a urologic oncology team, ideally within a clinical trial.
