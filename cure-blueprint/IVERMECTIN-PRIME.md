## Part 1 — The three breakthroughs

### Breakthrough 1 · The exposure gap is SOLVED (this was the fatal flaw — no longer)
- **Intravesical ivermectin: 1 mg/mL in 50% DMSO** (Rimso-50 is FDA-approved intravesical DMSO — regulatory precedent) + 0.5% chitosan + Tween 80, 50 mL, 60-min dwell = **~70× the tumor IC50 in dwell fluid** from a 50 mg dose. Urothelial IC50 is only ~15 µM and the 2022 urothelial authors explicitly proposed intravesical delivery.
- **Nanocrystals** (186 nm, **730× solubility**) and **platelet-membrane nanocrystals** (tumor homing, in-vivo TNBC efficacy, 2025) + PCL nanocapsules (70% glioma shrink at 26 µg/kg — carrier-driven).
- **A TAR-200-like depot insert** — the bladder-cancer benchmark is real: INLEXZO achieved **82.4% CR** in SunRISe-1 (JCO 2025). An IVM nanocrystal silicone insert is the natural analog.
- **Analogs change the calculus**: selamectin is **~10× more potent** than ivermectin (nanomolar, Sin3A-PAH2, in-vivo validated) · moxidectin has a **23-day human half-life** (90% GBM suppression in vivo) · SAR solved: spiroketal core = P2X7, disaccharide = P-gp — pick the analog by target.

### Breakthrough 2 · Ivermectin's true identity: resensitizer + immune primer, not cytotoxin
- **P2X7-dependent MDSC depletion** converts cold→hot (KN62 abolishes it) — and ivermectin potentiates *human* P2X7 specifically (rodent studies under-read the effect). Pairing insight: **N-803 (IL-15 agonist) upregulates MDSC PD-L1 — exactly the pool IVM depletes.** IVM lead-in → N-803/PD-1 is a mechanistically exact combination.
- **Human ICI data (real, modest):** NCT05318469 phase 1: 1 PR + 1 SD in 8 heavily pretreated TNBC; ICONIC (biomarker endpoint: Ki-67⁺HLA-DR⁺ CD8 expansion) opening. Phase 2 enriches PD-L1-negative.
- **Ferroptosis sensitization (new):** IVM blocks TMEM16F membrane repair, amplifying RSL3-class ferroptosis in vivo (2025). **Critical exclusivity rule: free ivermectin + artesunate is ANTAGONISTIC** — only a covalent conjugate synergizes.
- **Autophagy settled:** IVM is a true flux *inducer* (the contrary glioma paper is RETRACTED, 2026). Sequencing rule: **induce 24h first, block late** — blocker-first masks the flux load.

### Breakthrough 3 · The class verdict (honest)
Ranked by randomized human evidence > exposure margin: **1) artesunate** — the only repurposed antiparasitic with a randomized human recurrence signal (CRC: 1/9 vs 6/11 recurrences) + active RCT · **2) mebendazole** — brain Cmax 7.1 µM vs glioma IC50 0.1–1 µM (real exposure margin) · **3) ivermectin** — demoted to **chemosensitizer + ICI adjunct** unless delivered locally. The selection rule that settles all debates: *discard any drug whose achievable unbound exposure < tumor IC50* — which kills classic niclosamide, parbendazole, closantel, and oral ivermectin-as-cytotoxin.

## Part 2 — The companion diagnostic ($20, one biopsy)
p-HSP27(Ser78/82) Phos-tag/IHC + Yo-Pro-1 ATP-pore assay + P2RX7A:B splice ratio + ABCB1 + organoid response. **Decision rule:** treat if p-HSP27-high OR (P2RX7A:B >1 AND pore-competent); add chemo if ABCB1-high; **deny** if organoid IVM IC50 >10 µM or P2RX7B/J-dominant. Dynamic rule from Borealis-2: serum HSP27 must fall or rise ≤20.5% by cycle 2 (HR 0.29 in selected patients).

## Part 3 — IVERMECTIN-PRIME (the architect's protocol)

**Population:** BCG-unresponsive/high-risk urothelial carcinoma, biomarker-selected (panel above). **Safety gate:** ABCB1 loss-of-function genotyping (mandatory exclusion — coma case at 0.23 mg/kg), no dual P-gp/CYP3A4 inhibitors, baseline pupillometry + neuropathy exam (MMAE overlap with enfortumab is monitorable, ~38% AUC shift — no dose cut needed).

**Sequence (order is the mechanism):**
1. **Local anchor:** intravesical IVM 1 mg/mL in 50% DMSO/chitosan, 60-min dwell weekly ×6 (or TAR-200-style nanocrystal insert q3w)
2. **Immune prime:** oral IVM 30–60 mg 3 d/wk (fasted; food doubles exposure) — 7-day lead-in to deplete MDSCs → then atezolizumab/pembrolizumab ± N-803
3. **Biomarker-gated add-ons:** p-HSP27-high → docetaxel 75 mg/m² (IVM re-sensitizes via HSP27 + P-gp) · OCT1/3-high → metformin 1500 mg ER (p62-rise = flux engaged) · TMEM16F-intact + labile-iron-high → ferroptosis arm (RSL3-class; **never** with free artesunate)
4. **RT slot (if used):** IVM 0.6 mg/kg ~3h pre-fraction to block DSB repair (γH2AX-persistence biomarker) — with the radioprotection safeguard: verify γH2AX persistence, else stop IVM-RT overlap

**Kill criteria:** no γH2AX persistence → drop IVM-RT · serum HSP27 rise >20.5% → drop docetaxel arm · organoid IC50 >10 µM → deny IVM entirely · grade >1 CNS symptoms → hold.
